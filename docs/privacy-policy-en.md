# Privacy Policy — 3D Cube

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Korean version: [`privacy-policy.md`](privacy-policy.md)

Google Play requires a privacy policy at a public URL. Publish this file on
GitHub Pages or another public page and paste that address into the console.

---

## Summary

**This app does not collect any personal information.**

---

## 1. Information we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location,
advertising ID, or any other device identifier.

## 2. What is stored on your device

The following stay **only on your device** and are never transmitted anywhere:

- Solve records (time taken, scramble sequence, number of moves)
- App settings (language, theme, inspection on/off, sound, learning progress)

They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in system settings.

## 3. Camera

The app asks for camera permission **only** when you use the "scan a real cube"
feature. If you never open that feature you are never asked, and if you decline,
every other part of the app keeps working.

The camera feed is used **solely to read the color of each sticker**.

- We do **not** save photos or video. Nothing is written to a file.
- We do **not** transmit anything. The app has no internet access.
- The only thing taken from the image is which of the six colors each sticker is;
  that color layout is then used inside the app.

## 4. Internet access

This app does not connect to the internet. It does not request the internet
permission.

## 5. Sharing with third parties

None. The app contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics SDKs.

## 6. Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children
either.

## 7. Android automatic backup

Android can back up app data to your Google account. If that system feature is
enabled, the records and settings described in section 2 may be included in that
backup. This is a function of Android and your Google account — the app itself
does not send data anywhere. You can turn it off in your device settings.

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
